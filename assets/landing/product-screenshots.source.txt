product-composer-v1.webp:
The actual Monitoring visual composer, with a configuration accepted by
off_key_core.models.pipeline_compiler.compile_pipeline. Three inputs feed
standard scaling, parallel PyOD Isolation Forest / Local Outlier Factor
detectors, fixed-split calibration, the conformal alarm policy and evidence.
The current UI groups the calibration step into the detection-rule card.

product-evidence-v2.webp:
The actual Reporting reading-detail view, scrolled to its complete chart.
Illustrative cooling-circuit pressure, flow and temperature were supplied
through a local, unauthenticated preview fixture; no live systems or customer
telemetry were accessed. The example has 360 observations at 10-second
intervals, measurement noise, operating cycles, a brief pressure transient
and a developing restriction followed by recovery. The displayed evidence is
illustrative: Isolation Forest scores on synthetic inputs, ranked against
a separate reference window, with a restarting power-betting statistic.
It is not a runtime benchmark or a claim about detection accuracy.
The composer configuration and Reporting telemetry are separate examples.
